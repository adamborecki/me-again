    Playback

    Boosts each take to a loud level on save (no clipping). Recommended for the iPhone built-in mic.

    Extra trim on top of auto-maximize. Above 100% can clip. Also check your phone's media volume.

    Transitions

    The cue peaks at each section boundary: it rises over the last few seconds of one section and falls into the first few of the next, with a soft reverb tail. It overlaps recording and playback by design, so use headphones for clean takes. Reverse swell uses a plain swell until you've recorded something.
